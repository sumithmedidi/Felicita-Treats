Felicita Treats product image assets

37 product images are supplied as 768x768 WebP files, grouped by category. Filenames are stable, lowercase, and hyphenated. manifest.csv and manifest.json map each menu product name to its image path.

Visual direction follows the supplied stylesheet: warm cream and blush backgrounds, cocoa-brown dessert tones, and soft premium bakery presentation. The source image was generated as a full menu collage and each product tile was cropped into an individual image; these are individual files, not 37 independently generated renders. Review each crop before publishing, especially any tile where the crop may include a neighboring edge.
